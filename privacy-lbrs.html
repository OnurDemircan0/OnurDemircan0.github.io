Overview

Smash The Stones is an action-arcade game. No account registration is required — the Application can be used immediately upon download. The Service Provider is committed to minimizing data collection and being transparent about the limited data that is processed.

What Information Does the Application Collect?

1. Game Progress Data and Cloud Save

The Application saves game progress to your device's local storage, including your current level, gem balance, unlocked cosmetic items, and sound and vibration preferences. This data never leaves your device on its own.

To allow your progress to be restored if you reinstall the Application or switch devices, the Application uses platform-provided cloud save services:

  • Android: Your game progress is automatically backed up to Google Play Games Services (Saved Games), a service provided by Google. This requires you to be signed in to your Google Play Games account. Google may associate the save data with your Google account identifier.
  • iOS: The Application authenticates with Apple Game Center for platform services, and your game progress is automatically backed up to iCloud Key-Value Storage, services provided by Apple Inc. This requires iCloud to be enabled on your device and uses your Apple ID.

The data uploaded to these cloud services consists exclusively of your in-game progress (level, gem balance, purchased item states, and game settings). It does not include your name, email address, photos, or any other personal content beyond the game data itself.

The Service Provider does not have direct access to this cloud-stored data. It is stored and managed entirely within your Google or Apple account infrastructure, and is subject to Google's and Apple's respective privacy policies.

If cloud save is unavailable (e.g., you are offline or not signed in to a platform account), the Application continues to function normally and stores data locally on your device only. Local data will be lost if you clear the Application's data or uninstall without an active cloud save.

2. In-App Purchases

The Application offers a single consumable in-app purchase (a gem pack). All payment transactions are processed entirely by the Google Play Store (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS). The Service Provider does not receive, store, or process any payment information whatsoever.

After a successful purchase, only the resulting gem balance change is saved to your device and synced to cloud save as described above.

3. Information the Application Does NOT Collect

  • No name or email address (there is no registration or account system within the Application)
  • No analytics or usage tracking data
  • No precise or approximate location data
  • No camera, microphone, or contacts data
  • No photos or gallery access of any kind
  • No payment information (handled entirely by Google Play or Apple App Store)
  • No advertising identifiers or behavioral tracking
